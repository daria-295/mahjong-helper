# Mahjong Glossary

A short guide to the Mahjong terminology used throughout Mahjong Helper.

## Hand Structure

| Term | Japanese | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Tile | 牌 (Hai) | One of the pieces used to play Mahjong. |
| Meld | 面子 (Mentsu) | A group of three or four matching or consecutive tiles. |
| Chi | チー (Chii) | Three consecutive tiles of the same suit. |
| Pon | ポン (Pon) | Three identical tiles. |
| Kan | カン (Kan) | Four identical tiles. |
| Pair | 雀頭 (Jantou) | Two identical tiles. |
| Closed Hand | 門前 (Menzen) | A hand with no open melds. |
| Open Hand | Open | A hand containing at least one open meld. |

## Hand Analysis

| Term | Japanese | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Shanten | 向聴 (Shanten) | How many tile improvements a hand needs to reach Tenpai. -1 means complete, 0 means Tenpai, and 1 means one step away. |
| Tenpai | 聴牌 (Tenpai) | A hand one tile away from winning. |
| Ukeire | 受け入れ (Ukeire) | The tiles that improve a hand's Shanten, and the number of copies of those tiles still available. |
| Wait | 待ち (Machi) | The tile or tiles that complete a hand in Tenpai. |
| Winning Tile | 和了牌 (Agari-hai) | The tile that completes a winning hand. |
| Tile Availability | 残り枚数 | The number of copies of a tile that are still available, based on known tiles. |
| Discard | 打牌 (Dahai) | A tile thrown away from the hand. |
| Hand Efficiency | — | How effectively a hand moves towards completion, often measured using Shanten and Ukeire. |

## Winning and Scoring

| Term | Japanese | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Yaku | 役 (Yaku) | A scoring condition required to win a hand. |
| Yakuman | 役満 (Yakuman) | An exceptionally difficult hand worth the highest standard scoring limit. |
| Han | 翻 (Han) | A unit used to calculate a hand's value. |
| Fu | 符 (Fu) | Points awarded for certain hand features, used in scoring. |
| Dora | ドラ (Dora) | Bonus tiles that increase a hand's value but do not count as Yaku. |
| Riichi | リーチ (Riichi) | Declaring a closed hand in Tenpai by placing a bet. |
| Ron | ロン (Ron) | Winning using another player's discard. |
| Tsumo | ツモ (Tsumo) | Winning by drawing the winning tile yourself. |

## Hand Types

| Term | Japanese | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Standard Hand | — | Four melds and one pair. |
| Chiitoitsu | 七対子 (Chiitoitsu) | A special winning hand made from seven distinct pairs. |
| Kokushi Musou | 国士無双 (Kokushi Musou) | A special winning hand containing all thirteen terminal and honour tile types, plus one duplicate. |
| Yakuhai | 役牌 (Yakuhai) | Value honour tiles: dragons and relevant wind tiles. |

## Analysis and Simulation

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Probability | The likelihood of a particular event occurring, such as drawing a useful tile. |
| Simulation | Repeatedly generating or playing hypothetical hands to estimate outcomes. |
| Discard Recommendation | A suggested tile to discard based on hand efficiency, potential value and other factors. |
| Visible Tiles | Tiles known to the player, such as discards and exposed melds. |
| Neural Network | A machine-learning model that learns patterns, such as recognising Mahjong tiles from images. |
